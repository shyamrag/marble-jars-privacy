Marble Jars Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version. Marble Jars has online Rankings. Nothing is sent until you answer a one-time notice that appears when you win. Tap OK and your results are sent to our ranking server under a random nickname such as "Player 1234", and other players can see that nickname and your results on the leaderboards. Tap "Turn off" and nothing is sent. You can rename yourself, hide yourself, turn Rankings on or off, or delete all your ranking data in Settings at any time. The game works fully with Rankings off. There are no accounts, no email sign-up, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no payments.

Who runs Marble Jars

Marble Jars is run by Shyamrag Charuvil, an independent developer based in Naas, Co. Kildare, Ireland, who is responsible for your data under this policy (the "controller"). Contact: shyamragc@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your stars, best moves and times, level in progress, streak, daily jar record and settings (sound, vibration, colour symbols) are saved only on your device. So are a random secret that acts as your guest ID for Rankings, and your Rankings and "Show me to other players" choices. If your phone backs up apps (for example iCloud or Google backup), these may be included in that backup, which is between you and Apple or Google.

What Rankings sends, and when

Nothing is sent to our ranking server, and no ranking profile is created, until you turn Rankings on. The first time you win (a level or the daily jar), the game shows a one-time notice, "Your scores join the leaderboards", that says what is sent and that other players see your nickname and results. It has three choices:

  • OK: turns Rankings on. The game creates your ranking profile, sends your best saved results, and ranks the level you just finished (unless it was the daily jar).
  • Turn off: keeps Rankings off and sends nothing. The notice does not appear again.
  • Not now (or tapping outside the notice, or Back): sends nothing. The notice appears again on your next win.

You can also turn Rankings on or off yourself at any time with the Rankings switch in Settings. While Rankings is on, the game contacts our ranking server:

  • When you finish a numbered level (the daily jar is never ranked): that level's time, moves, undos and hints.
  • When you turn Rankings on, and each time you start the game, if you have cleared levels: your best saved result for every level you have cleared (time, moves, and whether a hint was used). This includes levels you cleared offline or before Rankings was on.
  • When you open Settings or the Rankings screen: to load your nickname and friend code.
  • When you use a Rankings feature: renaming, hiding, adding or removing a friend, getting a new friend code, reporting, blocking or unblocking, or deleting your data.

When you turn Rankings on, the game creates a ranking profile for you with a random nickname ("Player" and four digits) and a 6-character friend code. No name, email, phone number or account is needed.

If you used an earlier version: Rankings used to be off until you turned it on. If you had turned it on, it stays on and nothing changes. If you had switched it off, it stays off. If you never switched it on or off, nothing is sent when you update: you see the notice above on your next win, and your saved results are sent only if you tap OK.

IP addresses

Every request to the ranking server comes from your internet (IP) address. To limit abuse and automated requests, we count requests per address. We store only a hash of the address (for IPv6, of its first half), never next to your profile, and we treat that hash as your IP address because it could be reversed. Ordinary request counts are deleted after about two minutes. When a new ranking profile is created, a count is kept for 25 hours to limit how many profiles one address can create in a day. We do not use your IP address to work out where you are. Our hosting provider also handles IP addresses to deliver requests and keeps its own short-term technical logs.

What other players can see

  • On the Global, Country and Friends boards: your nickname, rank, levels cleared and total ranked time.
  • On the By level boards: your nickname, best hint-free time and moves for each level.
  • Your country is not shown, but you appear on the Country board of players whose device is set to the same country.
  • Friends you have added can also see your friend code.

Anyone who plays Marble Jars can see the boards, so treat your nickname and results as public. Do not use your real name, or anything that identifies you, as a nickname. If you turn off "Show me to other players" (or turn Rankings off), you are removed from every board, friends included, but your times still count, without your name, in the percentages and player counts others see.

Reporting and blocking

Tap another player's nickname on a board to report it or to block that player. We do not tell players who reported or blocked them. If three established players report the same nickname, it is replaced automatically with a default name, and we may also remove a nickname after reviewing a report. Removed nicknames are kept on a list, without any link to a player, so they cannot be used again. The game also refuses nicknames that contain web links, long numbers such as phone numbers, or certain offensive words.

Why we use your data, and our legal basis

We use ranking data only to run the leaderboards, friends and moderation features, and to keep the service free from cheating and abuse. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not sell it.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on our legitimate interests in offering the online rankings that are part of the game and in keeping them fair and free from abuse. We do not rely on your consent: the one-time notice is there so you know what will be sent and who will see it, and can say no before anything leaves your device. We limit what we collect (no real name, email or location), give every player a random nickname, and let you hide, turn off or delete your data at any time. You can object at any time by turning Rankings off or deleting your data, or by emailing us. Where we must keep or use data to meet a legal obligation, for example to answer a rights request, that obligation is our legal basis.

Who we share data with

Ranking data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. The database is hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase and its network providers may handle data outside your country, under Supabase's data protection terms and safeguards. Apart from other players seeing the boards as described above, we do not share your data with anyone, and we do not sell it or share it with advertisers. If you use the Share button, your device's share screen sends only what you choose, to whoever you choose.

How long we keep it

  • Ranking data stays until you delete it in the app.
  • Turning Rankings off stops anything new being sent and hides you from the boards, but does not delete earlier results.
  • Profiles with no ranked result for 12 months are deleted automatically (checked weekly), along with their results, friend links, blocks and reports.
  • IP address hashes: about two minutes, or 25 hours when a profile is created (see above).
  • Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's routine backups until those backups expire.

Turning Rankings off, hiding, renaming and deleting

All of these are in the game's Settings:

  • Rankings switch: turn it off and the game tells the server to hide you from the boards, then sends nothing more. Your earlier results stay on the server, hidden, until you delete them or they expire.
  • Show me to other players: turn it off to disappear from every board while still seeing your own rank.
  • Nickname: change it at any time.
  • Delete my ranking data: after you confirm, this deletes your nickname, country, results, friend code and links, block list and reports from the server straight away, and turns Rankings off. Your stars and progress stay on your device. If you turn Rankings on again later, you start a new profile and your saved best results are sent again.

You can also ask us by email to delete your data (see below).

If you remove the app without deleting

Your guest ID exists only on your device, so once the app is removed the in-app Delete button can no longer reach your data, unless you reinstall from a phone backup that brings the guest ID back. Your nickname and results stay on the boards until they are deleted automatically 12 months after your last ranked result. To have them deleted sooner, email us your nickname and, if you have it, your friend code and roughly when you last played. Default nicknames can be shared by several players, so we may ask for more detail and can only delete a profile we can identify.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your data, and object to how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself in Settings. For anything else, email shyamragc@gmail.com; we will reply within one month. Because there is no account, we may ask for your nickname and friend code to find your data. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Marble Jars is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over, and it does not ask your age. It is not directed at children under 13. Anyone under 16 should tap "Turn off" on the Rankings notice, or keep Rankings off in Settings, unless a parent or guardian agrees, and should never use their real name as a nickname. Parents can turn Rankings off or delete the data in Settings. If you believe a child's data is on our server, email us and we will delete it.

Support and reporting a nickname

To report an offensive nickname, tap it on a Rankings board in the game. For help with the game, to report a nickname another way, or for any privacy question, email shyamragc@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what is sent from your device, we will also say so in the game or in the app's release notes.
