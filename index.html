Marble Jars Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version. Marble Jars works offline and sends nothing unless you turn on Rankings. Rankings is optional. If you use it, your nickname, country and level results go to our ranking server, and you can delete them in the app at any time. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no payments.

Who runs Marble Jars

Marble Jars is run by Shyamrag Charuvil, who is responsible for your data under this policy. Contact: shyamragc@gmail.com.

If you do not use Rankings

Your progress, settings and any level in progress are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

If you turn on Rankings

Rankings is off until you switch it on in Settings. When it is on, we process the following:

DataWhy
Nickname (up to 16 characters)To show you on the boards
Country code, taken from your device's language and region settingFor the country board
Your best result per level: time, moves, undos and hintsTo rank results and check they are genuine
A random ID created on your device (we store only a scrambled hash of it)To recognise your device without an account
Friend code and friend linksFor the Friends board
Your "Show me to other players" choiceTo hide you from boards if you ask
Players you blockTo keep them off your boards and stop friend links between you
Nicknames you report, and nicknames reported about youTo review reports and remove offensive nicknames
IP addressTo limit abuse and automated requests

We keep your IP address only as a hash, and delete it within 25 hours (most entries within 2 minutes). Our hosting provider may also handle IP addresses in order to deliver requests.

We rely on your consent for Rankings, which you give by turning it on, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the service free from abuse for the IP address checks.

What other players can see

Other players can see your nickname and ranking results on the boards. If you turn off "Show me to other players", you are hidden from their boards, but your times still count, without your name, in the percentages they see.

Reporting and blocking

Tap another player's nickname on the Rankings boards to report it or to block that player. We do not tell players who reported or blocked them. If several players report the same nickname, it is replaced automatically with a default name, and we may also remove a nickname after reviewing a report. Removed nicknames are kept on a list so they cannot be used again.

Who we share data with

Ranking data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf and may do so outside your country under its own data protection safeguards. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. If you use the Share button, your device's share screen sends only what you choose, to whoever you choose.

How long we keep it

  • Ranking data stays until you delete it. Turning Rankings off stops new results but does not delete earlier ones.
  • Players with no ranked result for 12 months are deleted automatically, along with their results and friend links.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the game and tap Delete my ranking data. This removes your nickname, results, friend links, block list and reports from the server straight away. If you no longer have the app, email us with your nickname and we will help, or your data will expire after 12 months without a ranked result.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your data, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. To do any of this, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Marble Jars is a general-audience game and does not ask for your age. Children should ask a parent or guardian before turning on Rankings, and should not use their real name as a nickname. If you believe a child has sent us data, email us and we will delete it.

Support and reporting a nickname

To report an offensive nickname, tap it on the Rankings boards in the game. For help with the game, or to report a nickname another way, email shyamragc@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
